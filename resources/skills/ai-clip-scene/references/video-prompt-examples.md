# Примеры видеопромптов

Образцы стиля, не шаблоны: копируй подход, а не фразы, место или настроение. Обозначения входов в примерах условные («the first image», «the final image»): в промпте используй обозначения из указаний провайдера видео.

Две опоры, действие руками на ходу, ручная камера отступает перед героиней (6 с, монтажное окно 5,5 с). Приём: какой из двух похожих предметов участвует, порядок движений рук и что не меняется в конце:

> The first image is the first frame, the final image is the final state. One continuous frontal medium shot, 6 seconds. Handheld camera slowly backing away along the station platform in front of the woman at her walking pace, keeping the same distance and framing all the way to the final image. The train stays on the left of the frame, the row of benches on the right.
>
> The woman walks toward the camera at a steady pace the whole time, looking ahead past the camera. Her backpack hangs on her right shoulder. In her left hand, on the right of the frame, she holds two paper tickets, a blue one and a white one. From around 1 s, without stopping, her right hand pulls open the small pocket on the backpack strap at her chest. Then her left hand rises to the pocket, slips only the blue ticket inside and lowers again; by around 3 s the blue ticket is fully hidden. By around 4 s her right hand presses the pocket shut and drops, swinging freely with her steps until the end. The white ticket stays in her left hand, as in the final image; she does not put it away.
>
> Warm evening light from the platform lamps falls on her face and the backpack; the lamp reflections slide slowly across the train windows behind her.
>
> She is alive in every second of the shot, not a still image: steady breathing, natural blinking, a small sway of the shoulders with each step. Her lips stay closed, her face calm and focused.

Одна опора, неподвижная камера в интерьере днём (5 с). Приём: конечное состояние отдельным абзацем, единственная движущаяся среда названа, предметы разведены сторонами кадра:

> The first image starts the shot. One continuous medium shot in a sunlit kitchen, 5 seconds, static camera at table height.
>
> Thin steam rises steadily from the teapot spout and drifts to the left of the frame for the whole shot.
>
> The woman stands at the counter on the right of the frame with the white teapot in her right hand. From the first second she slowly tilts it and pours tea into the blue cup in front of her; by around 2 s the cup is full and she sets the teapot down on the counter, to the right of the cup. Then she wraps both hands around the cup, lifts it to chest height and slowly turns her head toward the window on the left of the frame.
>
> Final state: she stands in the same spot at the counter, framed from the waist up, holding the cup with both hands at chest height and looking out of the window, off-screen left; the teapot stands on the counter to the right of the cup. She does not walk away.
>
> She is alive in every second of the shot, not a still image: steady breathing, natural blinking, small movements of her shoulders; while holding the final pose she keeps breathing and blinking. Her lips stay closed, her face calm.

Две опоры, анимационный персонаж-животное, медленный наезд камеры (4 с). Приём: движение камеры приводит к кадрированию конечной опоры, одно ведущее действие и то, чего персонаж не делает:

> The first image is the first frame, the final image is the final state. One continuous medium shot of an animated forest clearing at dusk, 4 seconds. The camera slowly pushes in toward the small fox, ending on the closer framing of the final image.
>
> Fireflies drift slowly upward across the whole frame, and the grass sways gently in the wind.
>
> The fox sits on the moss in the center of the frame, ears up, looking at the paper lantern lying on the ground to the left of it. From around 1 s it leans forward and slowly nudges the lantern upright with its nose; by around 3 s the lantern stands upright and its warm glow lights the fox's face from the left, as in the final image. The fox stays seated; it does not pick the lantern up.
>
> The fox is alive in every second of the shot: its chest rises and falls with breathing, it blinks, its ears twitch and its tail curls slightly; while holding the final pose it keeps breathing and blinking.

Две опоры, общий план на природе, неподвижная камера (5 с). Приём: герой приходит из глубины к месту конечной опоры, среда движется на весь план, за героем ничего не появляется:

> The first image is the first frame, the final image is the final state. One continuous wide shot of a hillside meadow on an overcast day, 5 seconds, static camera at eye level.
>
> Tall grass ripples in steady waves in the wind across the whole frame, and low clouds drift slowly to the right.
>
> The girl runs down the path from the top of the hill toward the camera at an easy pace, her red scarf trailing behind her. By around 3 s she slows to a walk; by around 4 s she stops on the path in the lower third of the frame, slightly left of center, as in the final image, and looks past the camera, off-screen right. The path behind her stays empty.
>
> She is alive in every second of the shot, not a still image: her shoulders rise and fall with deep breaths after the run, natural blinking, the scarf stirs in the wind; while holding the final pose she keeps breathing and blinking.
