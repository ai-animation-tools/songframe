# Примеры видеопромптов

Принятые промпты пробы — образец стиля, не шаблон: копируй подход, а не фразы.

Две опоры, герой выходит из двери, ручная камера отступает (5 с):

> 图片1 is the first frame, 图片2 is the final state. One continuous medium shot, 5 seconds. Handheld camera backing away along the wet sidewalk in front of the man, with a slight natural sway, keeping him in a medium shot and ending on the framing of 图片2. The facade stays on the right of the frame, the railing and the empty road on the left.
>
> The man steps out of the workshop doorway on the right of the frame. In his right hand, on the left of the frame, he grips the old watch with the brown leather strap; his own watch with the black strap stays on his left wrist. In the first second he finishes the step onto the sidewalk and slowly turns his body toward the camera, his gaze moving from the left of the frame to straight ahead. Then, from around 1 s, he walks toward the camera at a steady, heavy pace along the facade, and the camera backs away at the same pace. On the way he walks past the amber workshop window on the right of the frame; by around 4 s the window is behind him, as in 图片2, and the door is further back. He keeps walking without stopping until the end, looking ahead past the camera, off-screen.
>
> Rain hits his shoulders, but the jacket keeps its dry texture; street lamp reflections tremble in the puddles. The door stays as it is.
>
> He is alive in every second of the shot, not a still image: steady breathing, natural blinking, slight tension in his jaw and brow, a small sway of the shoulders with each step. His lips stay closed, his face stays closed and tired, with no relief.

Две опоры, общий план сбоку, камера движется параллельно (5 с):

> 图片1 is the first frame, 图片2 is the final state. One continuous wide side shot, 5 seconds. The camera tracks sideways along the road at the same pace as the man, parallel to his walk, keeping the same distance, height and framing all the way to 图片2. The railing stays in the foreground, the facade behind him.
>
> The man walks from left to right along the facade at a steady, heavy pace, his whole figure in frame, looking ahead to the right of the frame. He stays on the sidewalk between the railing and the facade and never crosses the railing. In his right hand, the one closer to the camera, he holds the old watch inside his fist, only the brown leather strap hanging out; his own watch stays on his left wrist. He keeps walking without stopping from the first frame to the last, arms swinging naturally with the steps.
>
> As the camera moves, the facade slides to the left behind him: the two dark windows pass and give way to a blank wet wall with a drainpipe, as in 图片2. The railing posts slide past to the left in the foreground.
>
> The rain slowly grows heavier from around 1 s; by around 4 s the shoulders and upper sleeves of his jacket are visibly darker with water. Reflections of the street lamps and of a distant traffic light tremble in the puddles under his feet.
>
> He is alive in every second of the shot, not a still image: steady breathing, natural blinking, slight tension in his jaw, a small sway of the shoulders with each step. His lips stay closed, his face stays closed and tired, with no relief.

Две опоры, действие руками на ходу (6 с, монтажное окно 5,5 с):

> 图片1 is the first frame, 图片2 is the final state. One continuous frontal medium shot, 6 seconds. Handheld camera slowly backing away along the wet sidewalk in front of the man at his walking pace, keeping the same distance and framing all the way to 图片2. The facade stays on the right of the frame, the railing on the left.
>
> The man walks toward the camera at a steady pace the whole time, looking ahead past the camera. His jacket is already open. In his right hand, on the left of the frame, he holds the old watch with the brown leather strap. From around 1 s, without stopping, his left hand slowly pulls the left side of the open jacket aside. Then his right hand rises to his chest, slips the watch with its strap into the inside pocket on the left side of the jacket and lets go; by around 3 s the watch is fully hidden. By around 4 s his empty right hand comes back out, the jacket falls back open as in 图片2, and both hands swing freely with his steps until the end. The jacket stays open; he does not zip it.
>
> A soft green light from off-screen, behind the camera, falls on his face, the wet jacket and the puddles; warm street lamp reflections tremble on the wet tiles. Heavy rain keeps falling.
>
> He is alive in every second of the shot, not a still image: steady breathing, natural blinking, slight tension in his jaw and brow, a small sway of the shoulders with each step. His lips stay closed, his face stays heavy and closed, with no relief.

Одна опора, конечное состояние словами; камера только наклоняется вверх вслед за встающим героем, не приближаясь и не сдвигаясь (4 с, монтажное окно 3,2 с):

> 图片1 starts the shot. One continuous medium shot in a night workshop, 4 seconds, no cuts. The camera stays in the same place at the same height and never moves closer or farther; it only tilts up smoothly as he rises, keeping his head and shoulders in frame. The workbench, lamp and photo frame only slide down toward the bottom of the frame. By 2 s the tilt ends and the camera stays still.
>
> He has already made up his mind to leave; he does not look at the photo again. Right from the first frame, without a pause, he reaches with his right hand to the old watch with the brown strap and by 1 s picks it up firmly; his own watch stays on his left wrist. Then, holding the old watch, he stands up from the chair in one steady motion, right where he sat, and is upright by 2 s. Rising, he lifts his head, turns his face away from the workbench and looks off-screen to the right, past the camera, turning his shoulders on the spot the same way. By 3 s he stands still in a three-quarter view, eyes fixed off-screen right, framed from the hips up, the old watch in his right hand at chest height. He does not walk. No door or new part of the room appears.
>
> He is alive in every second of the shot, not a still image: he breathes, blinks naturally, his face and shoulders show small involuntary movements; while holding the final pose he keeps breathing and blinking. His lips stay closed, his expression heavy and resolved. Rain keeps running down the window.

Здесь конечное состояние описано внутри действия («By 3 s he stands still…»); можно и отдельным абзацем «Final state: …». Главное — кадрирование, поза, где руки и предметы, куда смотрит герой сторонами кадра.
